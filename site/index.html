The career. The peak. The present.

Greatness has
more than one measure.

One rating model is fitted to every recorded bout, then read three ways: what a career was worth (All-time), the best ten years (Prime) and form now (Current). All-time and Prime count every promotion; Current and the weight-class views count the UFC.

Every career has its own shape.

Era-adjusted rating after each bout, up to six fighters: each year is shifted by how far its contender line sat from the average year's, so careers in different eras share one scale. Later bouts revise earlier ratings, so an early-career point is read with hindsight.
