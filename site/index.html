Three ways to rank a fighter: the career, the prime, and now.

One rating model is fitted to every recorded bout, then read three ways: what a career was worth (All-time), the best ten years (Prime) and form now (Current). All-time and Prime count every promotion; Current and the weight-class views count the UFC.

Three boards, three questions.

The published boards. A weight class on the All-time board shows that class's board, earned in UFC bouts only. A blank Prime rank means the fighter did not qualify. Click a name to add it to Fighters.

An All-time score is three weighted parts.

1000 × (a·championships + (1 − a)·(s·career skill + (1 − s)·contender résumé)), each part scaled by its own top-100 mean. Move the rates to re-rank.
