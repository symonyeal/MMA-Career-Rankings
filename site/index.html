Symon UFC Rank Engine

Recorded MMA careers fitted together, then asked three different questions: what a career was worth (All-time), how good a fighter was at their best (Prime) and how good they are now (Current). Every chart below is one of the analyses in analysis/, drawn from the published snapshot.

The rankings

The published boards, filtered by the control room. A blank Prime rank means the fighter did not qualify, not that they placed last. Now in marks a fighter whose current class differs from the class they built their career in. Select a name to add it to Fighters.

Why the All-time leaders rank here

The All-time score is 1000 × (a·championships + (1 − a)·(s·career skill + (1 − s)·contender résumé)), each term divided by the mean of its own top 100. Bars reconstruct the published total exactly. Move the two exchange rates to re-rank the same eligible fighters; eligibility does not depend on them.

How far does one fighter's rank depend on the exchange rates?

Each cell is the fighter's All-time rank at that pair of rates. The ring marks the published rates. Select a cell to move the sliders there.

Fighters: career arcs and tale of the tape

Base WHR skill, fight by fight. Dots are bouts coloured by how they ended; the dashed line is the contender line. The arrow marks the best five-year stretch with at least the stated number of bouts. Cards compare what each career did; they do not predict a bout.

Where a career score comes from

Career skill mass sums, over a fighter's active years, how far their average rating stood above that year's bar: the contender line of their division, or the sport-wide line where the division is too thin. A year below the bar adds almost nothing; it never subtracts. The bars sum to the published career mass.

Height or duration

Mass is exactly active years × mean yearly excess, so every career sits on this plane and the dotted curves join equal mass. Up and left is a short, towering peak; down and right is a long stay above the field.

Does the order survive a higher bar?

The same career sum recomputed with the yearly bar raised from the field mean to its 95th percentile. Flat lines are fighters the choice of bar does not decide; lines that dive were collecting credit for years slightly above average.

Title lineage

Most dominant

Mean per-win dominance: strike differential, control time and submission attempts, combined. It is not a rating input.

Integrity policy view

A separate, stated judgement: fixed debits to base WHR points for PED-confirmed, disqualification and missed-weight wins. It never alters a published board and is not presented as a predictive improvement.
