UFC Career Rankings

Rank fighters by career (All-time), best ten years (Prime), or recent form (Current). One model rates every recorded bout. All-time and Prime include all promotions; Current and weight-class boards use UFC bouts.

Rankings

The published boards. A weight class on the All-time board shows that class's board, earned in UFC bouts only. A blank Prime rank means the fighter did not qualify. Click a name to add it to Fighters.

Score breakdown

1000 × (a·√championships + (1 − a)·(s·√career skill + (1 − s)·√contender résumé)), each part scaled by its own top-100 mean before the square root, so a career is paid for breadth more than for one extreme part. Ratings are read against their own era. Move the rates to re-rank.

Fighter comparisons

Era-adjusted rating after each bout, up to six fighters: each year is shifted by how far its contender line sat from the average year's, so careers in different eras share one scale. Later bouts revise earlier ratings, so an early-career point is read with hindsight.

Career scores by year

Career mass adds, for each active year, how far the fighter's average rating stood above that year's bar; no year subtracts.

Integrity policy

Judgement-based deductions for wins with a confirmed PED violation, a disqualification or a missed weight. These do not affect the published boards.

Forecast accuracy
